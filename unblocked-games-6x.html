Classroom 6X vs Unblocked Games 6X — they're not the same thing

The two phrases get used interchangeably, but they originally meant different things:

  • Classroom 6X was a Google Apps Script trick — embedding iframe games inside what looked like a Google Classroom document. The disguise was meant to fool teachers walking past your screen.
  • Unblocked Games 6X was a numbered fork of the UG 66 lineage, hosted at a domain that included "6x" somewhere in the URL.

UBGHyper does both jobs better: 720+ tested games (the UG side) plus a one-keypress tab cloak that swaps the page to look like Google Classroom, Khan Academy, Docs, Slides, or Desmos (the Classroom 6X side). One site, both behaviours.

How to play 6X-style games on a Chromebook

Most UBGHyper users are on school Chromebooks. Three quick tips:

  • Fullscreen with F. Most games support pressing F to enter fullscreen — useful on small Chromebook screens where browser chrome eats the play area.
  • Tab cloak on first load. Press the backtick key (`) as soon as the page loads. The tab title and favicon swap to Google Classroom (or pick from Docs, Khan Academy, Desmos in the dropdown). Even if a teacher looks at your tab list, all they see is school stuff.
  • Save export. Chromebook profiles sometimes get reset by IT. The /saves.html page lets you export your Retro Bowl / Cookie Clicker progress to a JSON file so you can restore it on a different Chromebook.
