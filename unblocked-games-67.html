What "Unblocked Games 67" actually is

Like UG66 and UG76, the "67" branding doesn't mean anything specific — it's just the sequence number of yet another Google Sites clone that students set up in the early-to-mid 2010s. The catalogues were nearly identical (same Flash games, same iframes, sometimes literally copy-pasted) but each site picked a slightly different number so it took school filters longer to find them. UG 6, 66, 67, 76, 77, 88, 99, 911, and dozens of others all came from the same playbook.

Today the 67-branded sites are mostly dead. The few that are still online are weighed down by aggressive ad networks (sometimes serving NSFW content), broken Flash links, and unmaintained codebases. UBGHyper is a from-scratch rebuild on github.io — same kind of catalogue, none of the legacy problems.
