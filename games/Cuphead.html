About Cuphead

Cuphead is a run-and-gun platformer built around non-stop boss fights, hand-drawn 1930s cartoon animation, and a difficulty curve that doesn't apologise for itself. You play as Cuphead (or Mugman in co-op) battling a series of contracts for the Devil — every level is a multi-phase boss encounter that demands you read attack patterns, dodge projectiles, and deal damage precisely under pressure.

What makes Cuphead different is its commitment to the bit. Every animation is hand-drawn and inked, every sound effect pulled from jazz recordings of the 1930s, and every boss has a personality and theme that matches its era. The result is something that feels like a lost cartoon from a time before video games existed — except it's extremely challenging to finish.

The difficulty is real. Cuphead doesn't hand out checkpoints between boss phases. You restart the full fight each attempt. Early attempts feel impossible; with pattern recognition, the same fight becomes manageable. The game respects the effort required to learn it.

How to play Cuphead

Controls. Arrow keys — move. Z — shoot. X — jump. C — dash. V — lock position (shoot in any direction without moving). Enter — pause. Hold a direction while shooting to aim.

Tips:

  • Learn the first two phases of every boss before worrying about the final phase — you'll never see the end if you're inconsistent early.
  • Parry the pink objects to build your super meter — they appear in almost every fight.
  • The spread shot deals massive close-range damage. On slow bosses, get in close.
  • Dying costs nothing — each death teaches the next attempt. Embrace the retry loop.

Frequently asked questions

How many bosses does Cuphead have?

Cuphead has 19 bosses across three main islands, plus 6 run-and-gun levels. The DLC adds 6 more bosses.

Can I play Cuphead in 2-player co-op?

The browser version supports solo play. The desktop version supports 2-player local co-op with Mugman as the second character.

What's the easiest weapon in Cuphead?

The Spread shot is the most forgiving — wide coverage, strong damage. The Charge shot is high-risk, high-reward for experienced players.

How hard is Cuphead?

Hard. Most players take 10–30 attempts on early bosses and more on later ones. The game is designed around learning through failure, not skill checks.
