About Monkey Mart

Monkey Mart is a cosy supermarket management game where you play a monkey running an ever-expanding grocery store. You plant bananas, harvest them, stock the shelves, and then watch customers grab your produce and queue at the checkout — where you ring them up and collect the cash to grow the business.

The clever part is the rhythm. You're constantly walking a loop: plant, harvest, restock the shelf that's running low, dash to the register. As money rolls in you unlock new stations — corn, eggs, coffee, peanut butter — and hire monkey assistants to automate the jobs you were doing by hand, turning the store into a self-running money machine.

Monkey Mart sits between an idle game and a management sim: active enough to keep you busy, automated enough to feel like steady progress. There's no failure state and no timer — just the satisfying climb from a one-shelf fruit stand to a sprawling supermarket with staff handling every aisle.

How to play Monkey Mart

Controls. Arrow keys or WASD — walk your monkey around the store. Everything else is automatic: stand next to a crop to harvest it, next to a shelf to stock it, or next to the register to serve customers. On touchscreens, drag to move.

Tips:

  • Never let a shelf sit empty — customers leave money on the table when there's nothing to buy.
  • Hire assistants as soon as you can afford them; automating harvesting and checkout frees you to expand.
  • Unlock higher-value products (coffee, peanut butter) to boost income per customer.
  • Upgrade your movement speed early — most of the game is walking between stations, so speed compounds.

Frequently asked questions

How do you make money fast in Monkey Mart?

Keep every shelf stocked, hire assistant monkeys to automate jobs, and unlock higher-value products like coffee and peanut butter. Income per customer rises as your store grows.

What are the controls for Monkey Mart?

Move with the arrow keys or WASD (or drag on touchscreens). Standing next to a station automatically harvests, stocks, or serves — there are no extra buttons to press.

Can you hire workers in Monkey Mart?

Yes. As you earn money you can hire assistant monkeys who automatically run stations like harvesting and the checkout, turning the store into a mostly self-running operation.

Is there an end to Monkey Mart?

There's no fail state or finish line — it's an open-ended management game. The goal is to keep unlocking products, hiring staff, and expanding your supermarket.
