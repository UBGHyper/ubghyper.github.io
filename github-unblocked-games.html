The fork-it-yourself path

Worst case: your school's IT specifically blocks UBGHyper. The github.io trick is that you can host your own copy on your own subdomain in 5 minutes. Here's the gist:

  1. Sign in to GitHub (it's free).
  2. Visit github.com/UBGHyper and hit Fork.
  3. Rename the fork to {your-username}.github.io (any username works).
  4. Go to Settings → Pages → enable GitHub Pages on the main branch.
  5. Visit {your-username}.github.io — your personal mirror is live in a couple of minutes.

This isn't a magic trick — it's just how GitHub Pages works for every developer. Your school filter sees a brand-new domain it has never indexed, and you've got a fresh URL it doesn't recognise. Use sparingly and with respect; the point is to give you a backup, not to start a war with your school's IT department.
