What Tyrone's Unblocked Games was

Tyrone's was a Google Sites-hosted unblocked library that spread almost entirely by word of mouth. Its appeal was familiarity and breadth — a big, recognisable catalogue that "everyone" knew the name of. As with the numbered sites, its weakness was maintenance: once Flash retired and the original stopped being updated, a large share of the catalogue broke and never came back.

Frequently asked questions

Is UBGHyper the same as Tyrone's Unblocked Games?

No. UBGHyper is an independent site and is not affiliated with Tyrone's Unblocked Games. It's a maintained alternative that offers the same kind of large unblocked catalogue, curated and with no pop-ups.

Is Tyrone's Unblocked Games still working?

The original, like most Google Sites unblocked libraries, is largely frozen or broken — much of its catalogue died when Flash retired in December 2020. UBGHyper keeps that kind of catalogue alive using Ruffle plus modern HTML5 games.

Does UBGHyper have the games Tyrone's had?

Yes — the Flash-era classics via Ruffle plus modern hits like Drive Mad, Slope, Cuphead and Retro Bowl. Over 720+ games in total.

Will UBGHyper work on my school Chromebook?

Yes. It is github.io hosted, which most schools allow, and every game runs in Chrome on ChromeOS with no extension or admin permission.

Does UBGHyper have ads?

Not on the game player — no popups, no video pre-rolls, no autoplay sidebar ads. Every game is hand-tested before being added.

How do I disguise the tab from a teacher?

Press the backtick key (`) or the camo button. The tab title and favicon switch to Google Classroom, Docs, Khan Academy or Desmos.
