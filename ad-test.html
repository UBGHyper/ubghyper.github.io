Adcash — In-stream Video (VAST pre-roll)click to play · VIDEO

network: Adcash · VAST tag r=11394930 · video.js + Google IMA · skippable pre-roll

This is the big one — your "pre-roll before the game" plan. Loads a video player and requests a pre-roll from Adcash's VAST tag (the ocean clip is placeholder "content"; in production the content is your game). Video CPM is far higher than display. Production: show this in a player in front of the game on ~1-in-4 loads, skippable after 5s. Needs Google's IMA SDK, which ad-blockers may stop — so it earns from non-blockers, but video pays enough that it can still beat all your display combined. If the player shows but no ad plays, the VAST tag didn't fill (try again / clean browser).

Tip: open this in a clean browser / incognito with no extensions to see true fill. Then open it in your normal browser to see how much your own ad blocker strips — that's roughly what your ad-block-heavy audience sees too.
